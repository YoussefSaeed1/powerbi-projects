# E‑Commerce Sales & Profit

> **Business question:** Which regions sell well but don't make money?

`Sales` · Aug 2024 · `Power BI` · `Power Query`

![E‑Commerce Sales & Profit](images/ecommerce.jpg)

## Key findings

- **$1.9M** sales, **25K** units and **$224.1K** profit for H1 2015.
- The South region sold **$357K** but **lost $14.4K**.
- May was the most profitable month ($67K profit on $306.6K sales).

## About the report

Monthly financial performance, profit by order priority, product category mix and a regional manager breakdown.
