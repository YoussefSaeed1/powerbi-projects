# Sales Analysis

> **Business question:** When and where do electronics sales peak?

`Sales` · Sep 2023 · `Power BI`

![Sales Analysis](images/sales-analysis.jpg)

## Key findings

- **$34.49M** in sales from **186K** orders across nine US cities in 2019.
- December is the peak month at **$4.6M**.
- Laptops and phones lead the product mix.

## About the report

Sales by city (map), quarter, month and top products.
