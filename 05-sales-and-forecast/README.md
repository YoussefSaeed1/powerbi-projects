# Sales and Forecast

> **Business question:** How did 2009 compare with last year and the forecast?

`Sales` · Oct 2023 · `Power BI` · `Forecasting`

![Sales and Forecast](images/sales-forecast.jpg)

## Key findings

- 2009 sales of **$40.62M** came in **$2.3M below 2008** ($42.92M).
- Sales beat the **$39M** forecast.
- The United States carries most of the revenue.

## About the report

Actuals vs forecast by country, top products, customers, brands and occupation.
